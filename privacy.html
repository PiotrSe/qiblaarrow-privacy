Privacy Policy for QiblaArrow

Last updated: September 27, 2026

QiblaArrow ("the App") is a mobile application developed by DevTrollo that helps users determine the direction of the Qibla using the device's compass.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

QiblaArrow does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect or store personal information such as your name, email address, password, or contacts.

Device sensors

The App uses the device's compass and motion sensors to determine the direction of the Qibla. Sensor data used for this purpose is processed locally on your device and is not sent to our servers.

Location data

If location access is enabled, QiblaArrow may use your device's approximate or precise location to determine your distance from the Kaaba and/or improve the accuracy of the Qibla calculation.

Location data is used only for the functionality of the App and is not stored by QiblaArrow on our servers. You can disable location access at any time through your device settings.

2. Advertising

QiblaArrow uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

AdMob may collect and process certain information from your device, including device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, and information about your interaction with advertisements, in accordance with Google's policies.

This information may be used to provide, personalize, measure, and improve advertising.

For more information, please see Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Data Storage

QiblaArrow does not operate its own server for collecting or storing personal user information. Information required for the operation of the application is processed locally on the device unless otherwise stated in this Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services, including:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements.
  • Google Play Services — for Android application functionality.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

QiblaArrow is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the App. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices

  • deny or revoke location permission through your device settings;
  • manage advertising preferences through your Google account and Android settings;
  • uninstall the App at any time.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
